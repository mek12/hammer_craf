# HAMMER-centered critical literature matrix

Search and access date: **2026-11-11**. The sole main paper is **R1 HAMMER**. R2–R5 provide comparative reading; R6 is an optional **preprint**. BibTeX keys remain `R1`–`R6`. This document contains no new experimental results for the project.

## Scope and search record

Inclusion criteria: image–text manipulation detection and grounding on DGM4; author-provided full text; official conference/publisher records for publication identity; official author repositories for code. Five published works and one preprint were selected. R1 and R2 belong to the same research line; they are not counted as evidence from six independent research groups.

Queries used: `"Unified Frequency-Assisted Transformer" site:link.springer.com`; `"Detecting and Grounding Multi-Modal Media Manipulation and Beyond" 2024 doi`; `"ASAP" "Advancing Semantic" site:openaccess.thecvf.com`; `"Unleashing the Potential of Consistency" site:openaccess.thecvf.com`; `"UFAFormer" github`; `"Fine-grained Multiple Supervisory" github`; `"Fine-grained Multiple Supervisory Network" journal conference`. The supplied arXiv identifiers were also opened directly. IEEE and the DOI resolver were tried for DOI verification.

Exclusions: secondary summaries, automated paper notes, and leaderboards were not used as technical evidence. The full text of additional candidate `2309.12657v2` was reviewed, but it was excluded from the final list to match the six source identities in the report and limit scope. When publisher full text was unavailable, the accessed arXiv version was explicitly identified; access failure was not treated as a scientific finding.

## Shared data and comparison notes

DGM4's [official dataset description](https://github.com/rshaojimmy/MultiModal-DeepFake#dataset-preparation) reports 77,426 original and 152,574 manipulated pairs. These are **dataset totals**, not 230k training examples. FS/FA/TS/TA counts are not mutually exclusive class totals because manipulations can be combined. R2 [III-D](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.14203v1) describes an approximate 7:1:2 source-ID split and text deduplication; exact train/val/test counts were not derived by multiplying the ratio by the total. Official metadata files were not counted during this literature review; exact split counts and identity of the local copy with the official version were **not verified**.

Metrics are separated by task: AUC/ACC/EER for binary detection; mAP/CF1/OF1 for types; IoUmean/IoU50/IoU75 for images; precision/recall/F1 for text. Follow the [R1 evaluation appendix](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02556v1) and [R3 §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.09667v1). Do not directly rank image/text subsets against the full test set, validation against test, or different pretraining/train+val protocols against train-only protocols. Superiority statements in sources apply to their own tables; no definitive cross-protocol SOTA ranking is established here.

Repository inspection reflects `main`/default branches as accessed on 2026-11-11; commit SHAs were not pinned. Code presence, checkpoint-link presence, and independent reproduction are three distinct verification levels. No independent training/benchmark was performed in this review. Comparative GPU-hours, peak VRAM, energy use, and multi-seed uncertainty were **not verified**; reported GPU counts are not interpreted as minimum hardware requirements.

## Limits of transfer to our project and open issues

The project's fixed intervention takes pre-fusion image and text CLS vectors from frozen/eval HAMMER; `final_logits = HAMMER_binary_logits + CRAF_delta_logits`. The final layer is zero-initialized; type/box/token heads are unchanged. This is neither gating inside attention nor new patch/token-level grounding learning. This scope comes from the user-provided project definition, not findings of the papers above.

**Interpretation:** The literature suggests questions motivating the intervention; it does not prove that CRAF is novel, SOTA, calibrated, or robust. Claims about the new method require its own experiments under matched protocols. No new project score is reported here.

Open issues: primary-metadata verification of R1/R2/R4 publication DOIs; direct publisher confirmation of R2 journal volume/issue/pages; R3 final-text/arXiv differences and actual implementation code; R4 v2/accepted-text differences and weight/auxiliary-data integrity; exact configuration matching between R5 released weights and paper runs; R6 peer-reviewed publication and official code; exact split manifests, repository commits, and independent reproduction for all works. Unknowns were not filled with estimated results or hardware claims.
